I am a final-year **Computer Science and Engineering** student at **Guru Nanak Institute of Technology** with a deep passion for the evolution of **Artificial Intelligence**. My technical expertise is rooted in **Full-Stack Development** and **Data-driven Applications**, where I specialize in building **API integrations** and **predictive data tools**. As a dedicated **AI enthusiast**, I am currently focusing my efforts on engineering **Agentic AI** apps and exploring **autonomous workflows** to build the next generation of intelligent tools.
